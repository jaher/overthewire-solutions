Bandit, level nine. The password for bandit ten is hidden inside a binary file, as readable text preceded by several equals signs. I log in as bandit9 with ssh bandit9@bandit.labs.overthewire.org -p 2220. Strings extracts the printable character runs from binary data, and I pipe it into grep for three equals signs. Four lines survive: the, password, is, and the password itself. That last line is the password for bandit ten, and the level is solved.
