Bandit, level eleven. The password in data dot txt has been rotated by thirteen positions, the classic ROT thirteen cipher. I log in as bandit11 with ssh bandit11@bandit.labs.overthewire.org -p 2220. Thirteen is half of twenty-six, so the same shift applied again returns the original text; ROT thirteen is its own inverse. I pipe the file through tr, mapping A to Z onto N to Z then A to M, and the lowercase range the same way. The output reads, the password is, followed by the password for bandit twelve, and the level is solved.
