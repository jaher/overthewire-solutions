Bandit, level sixteen. The credentials for bandit seventeen are served by one port between thirty-one thousand and thirty-two thousand on localhost, and I have to find which one. I log in as bandit16 with ssh bandit16@bandit.labs.overthewire.org -p 2220. I scan the whole range with nmap. Five ports are open: thirty-one thousand forty-six, thirty-one thousand five hundred eighteen, thirty-one thousand six hundred ninety-one, thirty-one thousand seven hundred ninety, and thirty-one thousand nine hundred sixty. Most ports just echo back what you send, but thirty-one thousand seven hundred ninety speaks SSL, and returns a key for bandit seventeen. I send the current password, read from its file, to that port with openssl s client, discarding standard error so only the answer shows. The daemon answers Correct, and prints a private key for bandit seventeen, from BEGIN to END, and the level is solved.
