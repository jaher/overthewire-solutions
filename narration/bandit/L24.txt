Bandit, level twenty-four. A daemon on localhost port thirty thousand and two asks for the current password and a secret four-digit PIN on a single line. I log in as bandit24 with ssh bandit24@bandit.labs.overthewire.org -p 2220. The note states the deal: get both right, and it hands over bandit twenty-five's password. The PIN space is only ten thousand values, so brute force is trivial: I generate every candidate from zero to nine thousand nine hundred ninety-nine, zero-padded to four digits, pair each with the password, and pipe all ten thousand lines through a single netcat connection. Grep minus v Wrong filters out the wrong replies. The daemon prints its greeting, answers Correct, and gives the password for bandit twenty-five, and the level is solved.
