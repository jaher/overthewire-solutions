Bandit, level twenty-four. A daemon on localhost port thirty thousand and two asks for the current password and a secret four-digit PIN on a single line. Get both right, and it hands over bandit twenty-five's password. The PIN space is only ten thousand values, so brute force is trivial. We first send one wrong attempt to learn the protocol, then generate every candidate line from zero to nine thousand nine hundred ninety-nine, zero-padded to four digits, and pipe them all through a single netcat connection. The daemon reads one line per attempt. Filtering out the wrong replies leaves the greeting and the success line with the password for bandit twenty-five. We verify it by logging in as bandit twenty-five.
