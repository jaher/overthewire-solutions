Vortex, level nine. This is the BlackBox: the official page has no information, no hints, no source. The goal is the vortex10 password. I log in as vortex9. I make a working directory and move in. The listing of /vortex shows the setup, and the level binary is missing: every other level N has /vortex/vortexN owned by the next user, but level nine's binary does not exist, and stat says no such file or directory. The next binary, /vortex/vortex10, is owned by vortex11 with group vortex10, and running it is refused, permission denied, so it is unreachable from vortex9. The group check explains why: the vortex10 group has no members, and setgid is refused, so there is no route to the level-ten binary at all. The password arrives by mail instead. find locates /var/mail/vortex9, the listing shows it ten bytes, mode six forty, owned by vortex9, and the read prints the password in plain text: nine five capital K s capital P one i three capital K. I log out of vortex9. Then I log in as vortex10 with that password. id confirms uid five thousand ten, vortex10. And the password file reads back the same password. The level is solved. I log out.
