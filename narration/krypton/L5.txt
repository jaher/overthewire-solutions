Krypton, level five. Another Vigenere cipher, but now the key length is unknown. The first step is to find it. We compute the index of coincidence for each candidate key length from one to thirty. That statistic measures how close a text is to English letter frequencies; it peaks when we slice the ciphertext at the true key length, because each slice is then a single Caesar cipher. The peak is at nine, and its multiples, so the key is nine letters. Then we recover each of the nine key letters by matching column frequencies to English, as before. All three found messages agree on the key. Decrypting the target and applying ROT thirteen gives the password, random. We verify by logging in as krypton six.
