Krypton, level three. This is a simple substitution cipher, and unlike the previous level we cannot query the encryption. But we have several intercepted English messages encrypted with the same key, so frequency analysis works. Across the corpus the most common letters are S, Q, J, U, B and so on, which is the shape of English frequencies. We build an initial mapping from the most common letters, then use a hill climbing attack that repeatedly swaps two letters in the mapping and keeps the swap when it improves the score. The score is the likelihood of the decrypted text under English four-letter sequences. The attack recovers the key, and the target decrypts to well done, the level four password is brute. We verify by logging in as krypton four.
