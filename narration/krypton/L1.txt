Krypton, level one. The password for krypton two sits in a file, encrypted with ROT thirteen. ROT thirteen shifts every letter by thirteen positions, halfway around the alphabet, so applying it twice returns the original text. I log in as krypton one, and read the file. The ciphertext reads YRIRY GJB CNFFJBEQ EBGGRA. I pipe it through tr, mapping the upper and lower case ranges onto their shifted counterparts, and it decodes to LEVEL TWO PASSWORD ROTTEN, so the password is ROTTEN. ROT thirteen is an encoding convention, not a real cipher.
