Krypton, level one. The password for krypton two is in a file, and the README tells us it was encrypted with ROT thirteen. ROT thirteen shifts every letter by thirteen positions, halfway around the alphabet, which means applying it twice returns the original text. It is its own inverse. We can decode it with the tr command, mapping the uppercase and lowercase ranges onto their shifted counterparts. The ciphertext reads level two password, followed by the password itself, which is the word rotten. We verify by logging in as krypton two. The point is that ROT thirteen is an encoding convention, not a real cipher.
