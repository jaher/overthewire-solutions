The executor moves the uploaded file, then checks its md five against the signature, and if they match it runs php on the file. The filename and signature are private properties, so we cannot set them through the form. But the upload lets us choose where the file is written, and that choice becomes the filename used in the check. Here is the trick. If the filename is a phar uniform resource locator, then the md five function opens a phar stream. Opening a phar makes php deserialize the metadata stored inside it. And the metadata is completely under our control. So we build a phar whose metadata is a serialized executor object, with a filename pointing to our own php payload and a signature equal to the md five of that payload. When php rebuilds the object from the metadata, its destructor runs at the end of the request.
