The README points at a backdoor hidden somewhere on the system. A find for setuid-root-style binaries sweeps the box and turns up two unusual entries: the level3 backdoor in var opt, and a level4 backdoor in var local whose name is a dot, six spaces, and level4 underscore backdoor.
