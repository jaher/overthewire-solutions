Blowfish, level one. This is the only step in the whole game that happens before you ever log in. The entry point is not SSH at all: the server on port six thousand and sixty-six hands you the level two password, but ROT13-encrypted. So the whole level is: connect with telnet, read the banner, decode it. Connecting to blowfish dot smashthestack dot org on port six thousand and sixty-six, the service prints Encrypted Password is, Gung Jnf Rnfl, and closes the connection. That is the entire service - no interaction, just a one-line banner. The cipher is ROT13, which means every letter is shifted thirteen places in the alphabet, and the tr command with the A to Z and N to Z A to M ranges is the one-line decoder. Feed the ciphertext through it: Gung Jnf Rnfl becomes ThatWasEasy. That string is the level two login password, and the very next level proves it - level two logs in with ThatWasEasy. Level one solved.
