Manpage, level zero. The goal is the manpage1 password, from /manpage/manpage0: a setuid manpage1 binary that calls setuid getuid, then strcpy's argv one into a 256-byte stack buffer. The setuid getuid call drops the effective uid to manpage zero, but the saved set-user-id still holds manpage one, so a setresuid of seventeen thousand one regains the privilege. The solve is a stack overflow with a shellcode. I log in as manpage zero. I read the source, cat /manpage/manpage0.c. I find the buffer on the stack with gdb: the EIP offset is 260, ASLR is off, and the stack is executable. I write the payload in python: a NOP sled, then a shellcode that calls setresuid seventeen thousand one three times and execve's /bin/sh, and the return address pointing back into the buffer. I run the exploit. id confirms uid seventeen thousand one, manpage one. And the password file reads back Mu eight G v M u d H b. The level is solved. I log out.
